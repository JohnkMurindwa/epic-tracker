Epic Stone App

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated data from Epic Stone. When your request is processed, we will permanently delete:

  • Your user profile (name, email, phone number)
  • Your authentication credentials
  • Your daily work logs and time entries
  • Your area assignments and clock-in/out history
  • Any photos or files you uploaded
Note: You can also delete your account directly from the app by tapping your avatar → Delete Account. This form is for users who can no longer access the app.
