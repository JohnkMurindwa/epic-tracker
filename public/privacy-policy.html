Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Epic Ceramic & Stone ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Epic Installation mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and role. Installers are assigned a 5-digit PIN for login.

Location Data: GPS location is captured only at clock-in to verify job site presence. We do not continuously track your location.

Work Data: Clock-in/out times, work descriptions, area assignments, and crew day records.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to verify job site presence, track work hours and crew day consumption, provide workforce reports to supervisors and administrators, and improve the App.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Your work data is accessible to your employer's supervisors and administrators for workforce management.

Data Retention

Account data and work records are retained for the duration of employment and a reasonable period thereafter. You may request deletion by contacting us.

Your Rights

You may access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Withdrawing location consent may prevent clock-in functionality.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will notify you by posting changes within the App.
