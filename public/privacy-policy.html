Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Epic Ceramic & Stone ("we", "our", "us", or the "Company") operates the Epic Installation mobile application for iOS and Android (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use our App.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Full name — to identify you in the workforce system
  • Email address — for account authentication (Supervisors, Foremen, and Admin)
  • Phone number — for contact purposes
  • Role — your position (Installer, Junior Installer, Helper, Foreman, Supervisor, or Admin)
  • 5-digit PIN — auto-generated for field workers to log in quickly on job sites

b. Location Data

When you clock in to a job site, the App captures your device's GPS coordinates to verify that you are physically present at the assigned work location. Location data is:

  • Captured only at the moment of clock-in
  • Not continuously tracked throughout the day
  • Stored as a single geographic coordinate (latitude and longitude)
  • Used solely for job site presence verification

You may deny location access, but this will prevent you from clocking in to a job site.

c. Work Activity Data

During your workday, the App records:

  • Clock-in and clock-out times
  • Lunch break start and end times
  • Work descriptions (selected from a predefined list)
  • Area and level assignments at job sites
  • Crew day consumption and progress records
  • Hours worked per area, per day

d. Device Information

We may automatically collect basic device information such as device type, operating system version, and app version for troubleshooting and compatibility purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

Data Purpose
Name, email, phone Account creation, authentication, and workforce identification
Role Routing to the appropriate dashboard (Installer, Supervisor, Foreman, or Admin)
Location (GPS) Verifying physical presence at assigned job sites during clock-in
Work activity data Tracking crew day consumption, generating workforce reports, and managing project timelines
Device information App compatibility, debugging, and performance improvements

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase cloud infrastructure, which provides:

  • Encryption in transit — all data transmitted between your device and our servers uses TLS/SSL encryption
  • Encryption at rest — stored data is encrypted on Google's servers
  • Authentication — access to data requires valid credentials (email/password or PIN)
  • Role-based access control — Installers see only their own data; Supervisors and Foremen see data for their assigned job sites; Admins see company-wide data

4. Data Sharing and Role-Based Access

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Within the App, different roles have different levels of access to your data:

Role What they can see What they can do
Installer / Junior Installer / Helper Their own clock-in/out history, crew days, and assigned area Clock in/out, take lunch, view their own work history
Foreman Worker names, roles, clock times, and work descriptions for workers at their assigned job site(s) only Assign workers to areas within their job site, view crew activity
Supervisor All worker data across all job sites — names, roles, clock times, descriptions, area assignments Assign workers and foremen to job sites, create areas, manage crew assignments across all sites
Admin (web dashboard only) All data plus financial information — labor costs, budgets, crew day consumption, project spending Create job sites, import project data from spreadsheets, track costs, manage the crew day rate, view company-wide reports. Accesses data through a web browser, not the mobile app.

Foremen can only see workers that the Supervisor has assigned to their specific job site — they cannot see workers at other sites or company-wide data.

5. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Your data is retained for the duration of your employment and active use of the App.
  • Inactive accounts: Data may be retained for a reasonable period after your last activity for record-keeping and legal compliance.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 7).

7. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete — request deletion of your account and all associated personal data
  • Withdraw consent — revoke location permission at any time through your device settings (note: this will prevent clock-in functionality)
To request data deletion or access:
Send an email to info@epicceramicandstone.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" or "Data Access Request." Include your full name and the email or phone number associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • The right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information
  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal information — we do not sell your personal information
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a workplace tool and is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will promptly delete their account and associated data.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data is processed and stored in the United States through Google Firebase infrastructure. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your data to the United States.
